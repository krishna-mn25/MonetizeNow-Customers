Data Migration

Migration Complete.
Exceptions Categorized.

All contracts processed from ENTR's signed PDFs and HubSpot deal records into MonetizeNow. Stripe customer IDs matched, invoices reconciled. A small number of exceptions fall into clear categories below. Once Gentry completes the invoice audit, open AR should reconcile against Stripe.

✓

All Good

The majority of accounts. MN contracts, invoices, and payment records match Stripe. No action needed. ARR is accurate and ready for reporting.

↔

Data Gaps Between MN and Stripe

Some accounts have invoices in Stripe that don't exist in MN (e.g., mid-period charges, amendment invoices created outside the original contract flow), or MN has invoices for upcoming renewals that Stripe hasn't billed yet. Resolution: confirm which system is accurate for each, then align.

⚙

Migration Configuration Errors

A few accounts where billing frequency (annual vs monthly) was misread from the PDF during migration, or payment amounts were incorrectly applied. Krishna is correcting these: rebuild the contract/invoice with the right billing schedule.

~

Historical Free Periods

MN doesn't have a native free period feature for historical contracts. These were modeled with an alternate approach (free period + new biz subscription = original contract length). Tradeoff: small $ differences on historical invoices created in MN vs Stripe. Stripe invoices + payments remain source of truth for those periods. Not a blocker, just a known FYI on how to read those records.

$

Out-of-Band Payments

Some accounts (e.g., Tyson) are billed entirely outside of Stripe via manual ACH to the bank. MN has the invoices; Gentry marks them paid with payment date notes. Not a system issue, just a process difference to keep tracking.

✗

Churned Accounts

Invoices for accounts that churned with outstanding balances were included in the initial unpaid report. These are expected and don't represent real open AR. Gentry to review and update account statuses in MN.
