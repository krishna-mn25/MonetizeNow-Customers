6 Five Decisions That Shape Everything
› Min Commit: Advance or Arrears?
Controls invoice timing, cash collection, and how many invoices per period.
Advance
Commitment invoiced upfront. Matches "included allotment" framing. Cash in hand sooner. Two invoices per period (advance + usage reconciliation).
Arrears
One invoice at period end. Simpler ops, single reconciliation. Usage + shortfall on same invoice. Easier for finance to audit. No upfront cash.
Our lean: arrears is cleaner for a first rollout. You can switch to advance later once billing is running. But if your finance team wants upfront cash recognition, advance is the right call.
› How Do Included Allotments Convert to Dollars?
MN min commits are dollar-denominated. Your tiers express allotments in units.
Math from overage rates
250K calls × $1.50/1K = $375. Workflows capped at $1K/mo. Total floor = $1,375/mo. Straightforward, transparent.
Custom dollar amount
Pricing team sets an independent commitment number that doesn't have to tie to overage math. More flexibility, less transparency.
Also affects display. If you want quotes to say "250K calls included" instead of "$375 commitment," that's a template customization. Billing mechanic stays dollar-based under the hood either way.
› Add-on Bundles: Same Contract or Separate?
Determines whether bundles are quote-time, amendment, or standalone.
On same quote
5th offering (Pre-paid Credits) on initial quote. Clean, single contract. Customer can't add mid-term without an amendment.
Mid-term via amendment
Customer starts without a bundle. Upgrades when they see overage building. More flexible buying motion.
Separate contract
Bundle on its own contract. Clean separation but two billing schedules, split reporting, renewal dates may diverge. Creates operational debt.
Key question: do customers buy bundles at contract start, or do they upgrade mid-term when overage builds? If mid-term is common, amendment is the right path. Separate contract compounds fast.
› What Identifier Does Your Metering System Use?
Determines subscription mapping approach for your eng team.
If internal customer ID
Store it as a custom field on MN Account. Your pipeline looks up MN subscription directly. No SFDC dependency. Recommended.
If SFDC Account ID
Bridge through SFDC → MN Account → Contract → Subscription. Works but adds a dependency on SFDC sync reliability.
This is for your eng team, not your business team. But it shapes how much integration work they're signing up for. Simpler identifier chain = fewer failure points.
› Migration: Amend at Renewal or Phased?
Determines how existing subscription-only customers get usage added.
Amend at renewal
When a customer renews, add usage offerings to renewal quote. Single contract, coterminous terms, clean ARR. Simplest path.
Mid-term amendment
For customers who want usage before renewal. Test proration behavior on sandbox first. Works but more complex quoting.
Depends on timeline. How many customers are getting usage products, and how fast? Natural renewal cadence = case-by-case, much simpler. All-at-once = we need a migration plan.
› Do Customers Need Real-Time Usage Visibility?
Determines whether MN's Customer Portal covers it or your team builds it.
Invoice-time only
Customers see usage on monthly invoice. No portal work needed. Works for low-consumption accounts.
Real-time dashboard
Credit balance, consumption rate, projected overage. We need to verify if MN's Customer Portal supports this natively, and if not, your team builds it.
For PE/VC firms on $50K+/yr platforms, consumption transparency matters. Doesn't block billing rollout but should be on the roadmap. Do you already have an internal usage dashboard you show customers today?
7 One Known Gap + How We Handle It
Credit & Rebill not available for usage tenants
If a usage invoice goes out with wrong data, there's no automated void-and-reissue today. Known product limitation. Interim: issue a Credit Note against the incorrect invoice, correct usage data, generate corrected invoice on next billing run, reconcile. Mitigation: build a pre-bill validation step so errors are caught before invoices go out, not after. We'll get a roadmap timeline from product on when this ships natively.
