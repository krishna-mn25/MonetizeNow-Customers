Advance or arrears?
Allotment translation
Your packages express included usage in units ("250K calls/mo included"). MN min commits are dollar-denominated. So Package B's 250K calls + workflow cap needs to translate to a dollar number. Example: 250K calls × $1.50/1K = $375 in call value. Is that number what your pricing team wants, or do they set an independent figure? We can make customer-facing output show units while billing runs on dollars under the hood. Just need to know which way you want it.

Invoice

Greater of actual usage or committed amount. Tax on usage + subscription only, not on min commit fee.
Arrears mode (simpler)
One invoice at period end. Usage lines + shortfall fee if under commitment. Customer used $400 against $625 floor → $400 usage + $225 shortfall = $625 total. Single invoice, single reconciliation.
Advance mode (cash flow)
Invoice at period start for full commitment ($625). End-of-period invoice shows usage lines + negative adjustment to avoid double-billing. Two invoices per period, but upfront cash recognition.

6Five Decisions That Shape Everything
Click any card for options and our lean.
› Min Commit: Advance or Arrears?
Controls invoice timing, cash collection, how many invoices per period.
Advance
Commitment invoiced upfront. Matches "included allotment" framing. Cash in hand sooner. Two invoices per period.
Arrears
One invoice at period end. Simpler ops, single reconciliation. Usage + shortfall on same invoice. No upfront cash.
Our lean: arrears is cleaner for a first rollout. You can switch to advance later once billing is running.
› How Do Included Allotments Convert to Dollars?
MN min commits are dollar-denominated. Your tiers express allotments in units.
Math from overage rates
250K calls × $1.50/1K = $375. Workflows capped at $1K/mo. Total floor = $1,375/mo. Straightforward.
Custom dollar amount
Pricing team sets an independent commitment number. More flexibility, less transparency.
Also affects display. If quotes should say "250K calls included" instead of "$375 commitment," that's a template customization. Billing stays dollar-based either way.
› Add-on Bundles: Same Contract or Separate?
Determines whether bundles are quote-time, amendment, or standalone.
On same quote
5th offering on initial quote. Single contract. Can't add mid-term without amendment.
Mid-term via amendment
Customer starts without a bundle. Upgrades when overage builds. More flexible buying motion.
Separate contract
Bundle on its own contract. Clean separation but two billing schedules, split reporting. Creates operational debt.
Key question: do customers buy bundles at contract start, or mid-term when overage builds?
› What Identifier Does Your Metering System Use?
Determines subscription mapping approach for eng.
If internal customer ID
Store as custom field on MN Account. Direct lookup. No SFDC dependency. Recommended.
If SFDC Account ID
Bridge through SFDC → MN. Works but adds dependency on SFDC sync reliability.
› Migration: Amend at Renewal or Phased?
How existing subscription-only customers get usage added.
Amend at renewal
Add usage offerings to renewal quote. Single contract, coterminous terms, clean ARR.
Mid-term amendment
For customers who want usage before renewal. Test proration on sandbox first.
Depends on timeline. How many customers are getting usage products, and how fast?
› Do Customers Need Real-Time Usage Visibility?
Determines whether MN portal covers it or your team builds it.
Invoice-time only
Customers see usage on monthly invoice. No portal work.
Real-time dashboard
Credit balance, consumption rate, projected overage. We need to verify if MN portal supports this natively.
Doesn't block billing rollout. But for PE/VC firms on $50K+ platforms, consumption transparency matters. Do you already show customers a usage dashboard today?
7One Known Gap + Tax Behavior
7 out of 8 capabilities ready. One gap with an interim process.
Credit & Rebill not available for usage tenants
If a usage invoice goes out with wrong data, there's no automated void-and-reissue today. Known product limitation. Interim: issue a Credit Note against the incorrect invoice, correct usage data, generate corrected invoice on next run. Mitigation: build a pre-bill validation step so errors are caught before invoices go out. We'll get a roadmap timeline from product on when this ships natively.
