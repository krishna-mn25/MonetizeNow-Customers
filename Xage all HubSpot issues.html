Line item sync → HubSpot deal
The core pain
MN sends qty=1 + price=blob to native HS fields Structural ›
Real values exist in monetize_quoteitem_* custom properties but native HS fields show meaningless data. This is why Xage hired SimpleStrat to build a corrective workflow.
Heather's reporting, forecasting, and pipeline all run on native HS fields. "1 × $127,500" tells her nothing.
#1150, #3662, #3701
SimpleStrat workflow strips MN dedup keys → duplicates Active ›
Workflow corrects native values but deletes + recreates line items, losing monetize_quoteitem_id and monetize_quoteoffering_id. Next MN sync can't find matches → creates duplicates.
Confirmed by Huy Ngo (MN eng) May 14: "If the fields that we depend on have their values removed, we can't deduplicate so we end up creating more line items."
#4645
MN re-syncs after quote is processed → overwrites corrections Active ›
Xage's process: close deal as Won first → then process quote in MN. MN fires webhook on Processed → full line item resync → overwrites what SimpleStrat just fixed.
May 11 fix agreed: trigger workflow AFTER Processed. But Heather has to manually move quotes to Processed due to co-signer step blocker.
#4498
Quote expiration triggers re-sync on closed deals Active ›
Heather reported May 27: an expired quote re-synced and doubled line items + amount on a closed deal.
This breaks the "sequence after Processed" fix — expiration can fire at any time on any linked quote and re-trigger the whole overwrite chain.
